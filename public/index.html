Questions

Before you join the clowder

Is I Love Meow free? +

Yes — posting, browsing, and joining clowders are free. An optional Season Pass adds cosmetic profile decorations and nothing pay-to-win.

Can I add more than one cat? +

As many as you have. Each cat gets its own profile, gallery, and timeline, all linked to your account.

What's a Fish Coin? +

Our in-app currency, earned through streaks and challenges. Spend it on profile decorations and event entries.

Can shelters post adoptable cats? +

Yes — verified shelters and rescues get a dedicated adoption listing type with location and application forms.
